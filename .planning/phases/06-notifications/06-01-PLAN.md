<objective>
Implement the Rust notification backend: config fields for mute/DND, `send_notification` command with active/mute/DND guards, `toggle_mute_app` and `set_dnd` commands, `tauri-plugin-notification` integration, capability scoping, and `window.Notification` intercept in the initialization script.

Purpose: This is the entire Rust-side notification pipeline. App webviews will intercept `new Notification()` calls and route them through Rust, which decides whether to fire an OS notification based on active app, mute, and DND state.

Output: Working notification bridge from webview to OS. Mute/DND state persisted in apps.json.
